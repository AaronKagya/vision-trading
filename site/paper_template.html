Why these numbers are trustworthy—and what they don't prove

The archived 2025 Q1 forecast file was checked against the exact published policy, including terminal liquidation cost. The 2025 Q2 results were validated against the five-seed 2024-only epoch selection. This replay is not a fresh untouched backtest: 2025 Q1 and Q2 have already been examined. Scores omit actual bid/ask, slippage, execution constraints, margin, funding, latency, downtime and short borrow mechanics. Even accurate historical models can lose money.
