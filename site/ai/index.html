What must happen before this becomes a trading product?

We need enough independent, truly forward-recorded outcomes; comparison against simple baselines; uncertainty calibration; a new independent sample; and properly modeled execution costs. A high accuracy percentage by itself does not establish a profitable strategy. The simultaneous historical 2024/2025 analyses are separate from this new prospective ledger.
