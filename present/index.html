Likely examiner questions

Why compare chart images and numerical inputs?

To measure whether alternative representations and model architectures help extract useful patterns from the same historical prices. Deterministically generated chart images do not add independent raw information.

How did you prevent look-ahead leakage?

Trailing windows, strict timestamp ordering, expanding walk-forward folds, embargo between train/validation/test partitions, training-only scaling and tests for causal indexing. No model may use the unknown future outcome as an inference input.

Why is accuracy above 50% not enough?

Class imbalance, overlapping labels, market nonstationarity, returns asymmetry and transaction costs can prevent positive economic performance. The 2025 holdouts show why external testing matters.

Is the model already trading with real money?

No. We have an interactive historical replay and forward-only observational monitors. The cloud paper monitor is rule based; the separate frozen-model shadow audit does not place orders.
