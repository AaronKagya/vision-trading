Research integrity and next steps

Equal 15-minute sampling and training settings make a useful controlled comparison, but do not make cryptocurrency results independent. Prediction accuracy alone does not demonstrate trading profitability. The illustrative transaction-cost assumption has not been validated across instruments.

The initial BTC control is the original completed seed-0 experiment. Any ETHUSDT and SOLUSDT estimates are published only after GitHub finishes the same four-fold evaluation. Five-seed comparisons are clearly distinguished from single-seed pilots. Cross-market transfer (train on BTC, test on ETH) is a separate experiment—not established by individually trained models.
